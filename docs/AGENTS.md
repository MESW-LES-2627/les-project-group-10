# Documentation

- Keep project-wide documents, such as deployment, design and the definition of done, at the top level.
- Put feature guides and their acceptance notes together in a feature folder: `payments/`, `profile/`, or another clear name.
- Keep iteration records and their images in `iterations/`.
- Use short, clear filenames and simple wording. Explain what works and any important limits.
- When moving a document, update its links and all references to its old path.
- Keep the documentation index in `README.md` up to date. Do not duplicate existing guides.
